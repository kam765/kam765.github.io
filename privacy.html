Privacy

Y5S1 Study Hub · last updated 1 October 2026

Who runs it

The Study Hub is a Google Apps Script web app run by Kamil Kubizna (kamilkubizna@gmail.com) for a small group of classmates. Only people on its member list can use it.

What Google shares when you sign in

Only your email address (and an anonymous Google account number that stops someone else taking over your place). The hub uses them to check you're on the member list. It doesn't ask for your name or picture, and it doesn't use your Google Drive, Gmail, calendar or contacts.

What the hub stores

  • What you add: questions, answers, tips, links, deadlines, progress ticks, study sessions, thesis card and goals, uploaded files.
  • When you last signed in.
  • Anonymous usage counts (how often each page is opened, never who opened it).

Everything is stored in a Google Sheet and Google Drive folders in Kam's Google account. The hub runs in Kam's account, so Kam's account can open all of it, including items only you see in the hub. The hub itself never shows your own quick links to Kam or anyone else; feedback you send is for Kam, so he sees it. Most of what you add is visible to the other members, as the hub says where you add it. The data sheet and its backups are shared with nobody; the course folders and Our Notes folders are shared only with people on the member list.

What leaves Google

The hub talks only to Google services. Signing in uses Google's own sign-in service; on the way back your browser passes through a small page on this site (kam765.github.io) that hands the sign-in reply straight to the hub and stores nothing. GitHub, which hosts this site, sees the visit like any website host; the one-time code in it is useless without the hub's secret. Google names this site on its sign-in screen: that's expected.

How long it's kept

For the academic year. Deleted items stay in the hub's bin for 30 days; daily backups are kept for 14 days. When the group stops using the hub, Kam deletes the data.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time (More › Account), on this device or everywhere.
  • Delete what you added from inside the hub, or ask Kam to remove you and your data.
  • If you used the hub before October 2026 you gave it a broader Google permission, which it no longer uses. Remove it at Google Account › Security › Third-party connections: pick the old Y5S1 Study Hub entry (the one with Drive access) and delete its connections. The sign-in one (email address only) stays.

Checking it yourself

Every live version of the code gets an independent security review, published to the members in the hub's Security & source page, and members can read the code.
